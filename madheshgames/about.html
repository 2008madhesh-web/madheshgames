About us

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Gridplay is a free website where you can play browser games instantly, on any device, with no downloads and no accounts.

What we do

We collect fun, easy-to-start games across puzzle, racing, sports, action, strategy and casual categories, and organise them so you can find something to play in seconds.

Our approach

We aim to keep games working, pages fast and the site safe to use. We review games before listing them and remove any that break, misbehave or do not belong.

How we pay for it

Gridplay is free to use. We show ads from Google AdSense to cover hosting and the time it takes to add new games. Ads are always labelled as advertisements.
